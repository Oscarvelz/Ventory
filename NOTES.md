Install **Ventory.apk** on the Android phone or tablet (Android 11 or newer).

- The app opens https://oscarvelz.github.io/Ventory/ with its own storage, separate from Chrome: sign in once with your Ventory account and your floor comes back by sync. It works offline after the first open.
- Back closes the open sheet; on the main screen press Back twice to leave. The status and navigation bars follow the theme. Backups you export are saved in Downloads › Ventory. Piece photos use the camera or the photo picker.
- When a new version is published, the app offers to install it.
